Recipe — Egg Bhurji (Anda Bhurji)

Serves 2 · ~20 min.

Ingredients: 4 eggs; 2 tbsp oil; 1/2 tsp cumin; 1 small onion (chopped); 1–2 green chilies (opt); 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste; 1 small tomato; 1/4 tsp turmeric; 1/2 tsp red chili powder; 1/2 tsp garam masala; 2–3 tbsp coriander; salt.

  1. Beat eggs with pinch of salt.
  2. Heat oil, sizzle cumin; cook onion + chilies 2–3 min.
  3. Add ginger-garlic 30–60s; add tomato 2–3 min; add turmeric + chili 10–20s.
  4. Lower heat, pour eggs, stir breaking curds 1–2 min — pull off while slightly soft.
  5. Add garam masala + coriander, adjust salt. Serve with roti/paratha/pav/rice.

Journaling prompts (5–10 min, no editing)

  1. The thing left unsaid: "What did I wish I'd said or heard before it ended — with no obligation to send it?"
  2. What was real: "What parts felt real/good, and what parts left me uneasy? List both, no silver lining."
  3. What I need right now: "If I treat myself as a friend right now, what do I actually need in the next 2–3 hours — food, rest, noise, silence, company?"
  4. Proof that's not this: "When have I felt known by someone (even briefly) in recent years? What did that feel like, exactly?"
  5. A small boundary for today: "What am I not obligated to do over the next 48 hours (ruminate, check, explain)?" Pick one to stop today.

Short-term mood practices

  • Sunlight: 10–20 min outside/balcony (shade fine in Ahmedabad sun).
  • Movement: 10-min walk (5 min if low energy).
  • Cold water (gentle): splash face/wrists 30–60s or brief cooler shower end.
  • Music: one song you actually liked before; listen fully.
  • Breathing: box 4-4-4-4 for 1–5 min, or slow exhale longer than inhale.
  • Sleep basics: cool/dark-ish room, phone away 10 min before bed, consistent-ish wake.

If it gets heavy

  1. Safety first: if you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 112 (India) or go to the nearest emergency department.
  2. Reach out: "Can I sit on call with you for a bit?" is enough.
  3. Pause: water, slow breathing (4 in, 6 out), cold water on face, step outside for air if possible.
